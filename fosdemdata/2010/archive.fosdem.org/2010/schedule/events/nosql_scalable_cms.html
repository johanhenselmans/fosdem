Designing a scalable content management system on NoSQL technologies

This talk discusses what it takes to design a scalable and searcheable content management system.

  • Which design decisions do we take?
  • What NoSQL technologies do we choose and why?
  • How do we model a CMS schema on top of them?
  • What are the challenges we face to provide CMS and search functionality in a scalable and highly available environment?
