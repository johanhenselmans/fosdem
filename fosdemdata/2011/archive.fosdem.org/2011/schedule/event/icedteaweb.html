What in the World Is This 'IcedTea-Web' Project?

This talk will cover the new !Icedtea-Web project -- what it is, its current status, and the future direction for it.

  • What is IcedTea-Web, and why the need for it?
  • The current status of IcedTea-Web
    • Info on the included items (Plugin, NetX, ControlPanel)
    • Synopsis of the new features in Plugin and NetX
  • The future goals of the project
  • Some demos

By the end of the talk, you will:

  1. Never again wonder what the heck IcedTea-Web is
  2. Be well refreshed after the nap you took 5 minutes after the talk started :)
