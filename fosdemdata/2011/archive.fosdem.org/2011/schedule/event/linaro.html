Linaro

Linaro is a not-for-profit organization funded by ARM, Texas Instruments, Freescale, ST-Ericsson, Samsung and IBM for the success of opensource on ARM. With more than 90 full time engineers in areas as diverse as toolchain, graphics, multimedia, kernel, power management, platforms and many more, we aim at improving and consolidating the lower levels of Linux based stacks in future products.

Come see all the good Linaro outputs you can take home, get a breath of our freshest development plans, and a taste of how we work internally and with upstreams and downstreams. Discover some of the tools we've improved or created, and the ones coming next. Challenge your views of what makes a modern Linux based OS now, and what it might be in the future.
