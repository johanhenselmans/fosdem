Map:




To access Plaine campus:

    Landmarks: Delta station, Ixelles graveyard.

    From Gare Centrale

    Take autobus 71 direction Delta. Step down at Delta. It is also possible to arrive by underground (direction Debroux, switch at Mérode Station, step down at Delta).

     
    From Gare du Midi

    Take the underground direction Simonis. Step down at Porte de Namur station, then take autobus 71 direction Delta. Step down at Delta. It is also possible to arrive by just taking the underground (instead of stepping down at Porte de Namur station, just continue and switch at Arts-Loi, then direction Debroux, switch at Mérode station, step down at Delta).
     

    From Gare du Nord

    Take autobus 61 (direction Montgomery) to Mérode. There, take the underground, direction Debroux, to Delta.
     

    From Gare d'Etterbeek

    By leaving the station, turn right on boulevard Général Jacques, then take on your right boulevard de la Plaine and follow it towards the Campus Plaine entrances.
     

    Other possibilities

    Ten minutes walking from Solbosch campus, following avenue de l'Université.

    By car: direct acces by Namur-Brussels motorway (E411). ?>
