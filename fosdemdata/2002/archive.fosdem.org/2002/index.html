FOSDEM 2002 is coming February 16-17!
The next edition will take place in Brussels on February 16 & 17, 2002! Planning is well under way and it's going to be a great event with free entrance (but please sign up). We look forward to seeing you there.
If you're looking for accomodation, look at our corresponding forum.
If you took pictures during the event, please let us know at pics@fosdem.org. Put your pictures online and send us the link to see them!
During the week-end, you can also check the FOSDEM wiki.
Now that FOSDEM 2002 is over, you can also go to the Post-discussion forum to discuss about the event.
