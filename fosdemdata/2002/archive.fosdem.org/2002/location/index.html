So, coming to FOSDEM is something really easy ... this can be done using railways, car, and so on ...


By car:

From Namur (E411)
At the end of the motorway, facing ULB, take left ; then turn left at the first street on the 'Fraiteur' bridge'. Going straight forward (avenue de l'Université), You can't miss the Solbosch campus.

From Liège (E40)
At the end of the motorway, follow 'Bruxelles Sud' indications. At the end of the road 'Boulevard General Jacques', take left 'avenue Buyl' or 'avenue Franklin Roosevelt'..

From Paris-Mons (E19)
Leave motorway at Drogenbos and follow 'centre-ville' indication. At 'avenue Brugmann', take 'avenue Defré' and cross the 'chaussée de Waterloo' and enter the 'bois de la Cambre'. Take first on your right in the wood and take left at the first crossroad, the immediatly right. Go forward to reach 'avenue Franklin Roosevelt', then go left to reach the campus.

From Oostende (E40)
Follow 'centre ville' indications, the the tubes to the indication 'Bois de la Cambre'. Take the tube on the right and follow 'avenue Louise' that continue as 'avenue Franklin Roosevelt' where stands the campus.

From Antwerpen
Take the RO direction (East) to junction with Liège motorway. Follow 'Bruxelles Sud' indication and at the end of 'Boulevard General Jacques', take 'avenue Buyl' or 'avenue Franklin Roosevelt' on your left.


By train:

From Gare Centrale (Central Station)
Take bus 71 direction Delta. Step down at ULB-Solbosch stop.

From Gare du Midi (South Station)
Take the metro direction Simonis. Step down at 'Louise' station. Take tramway 93 or 94, direction Wiener, to Solbosch.
Other soution : Step down at 'Porte de Namur' station, then take bus 71 direction Delta and step down at ULB-Solbosch.

From Gare du Nord (North Station)
Take bus 61 (direction Montgomery) or 38 (direction Homborch) to 'Botanique'. Then take tramway 93 or 94, direction 'Wiener' to Solbosch.

From Gare d'Etterbeek (Etterbeek Station)
Take bus 95 (direction Heiligenborre) or 96 (direction Sainte-Anne) to Ixelles cemetery. From that point, it takes less than 5 minutes by foot, following 'avenue de l'Université' to Solbosch.


You can also take tramway 23 and 90 to 'Place de l'Etoile'.
