Event Track Day Room Start time Duration
Where's OOo's UI abstraction heading for? OpenOffice.org Saturday AW1.117 15:15 01:00

Thorsten Behrens

Thorsten Behrens, being a programmer since ages, finished a degree in computer science; and since winter 2003, he's been busy redesigning and implementing OOo's new rendering and slideshow components.

Thorsten is happy to be a member of Novell's awesome OpenOffice.org team.
