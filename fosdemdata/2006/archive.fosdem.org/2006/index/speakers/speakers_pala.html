Massimiliano Pala

OpenCA Project Manager and Chief Designer

Project:

OpenCA
Biography

Massimiliano Pala holds an M.S. Degree in Computer Engineering. He is attending PhD course at the Politecnico di Torino in PKI and Security area, currently he is visiting PhD at Dartmouth College. He started the OpenCA project in 1998 and still continues its development and management. His research interests are in the field of new security architectures and protocols development, Peer-to-Peer technologies for PKIs and e-Government projects.
