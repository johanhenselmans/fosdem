Welcome to the brand new FOSDEM site !

The third Free and Open source Software Developers' European Meeting is a 2 days event, organized by volunteers, to promote the widespread use of Free and Open Source software. Taking place in the beautiful city of Brussels, Belgium FOSDEM meetings are recognised as the best Free and Open Source events in Europe.


Important Note: Entrance and attendance to FOSDEM talks and tutorials are free, but donations to help us organizing and keeping the event free are welcome, please see here.

If you take pictures during the FOSDEM, send them to pics@fosdem.org and we'll make them available on http://fosdem.3ti.be
