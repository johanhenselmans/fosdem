[ 03-03-2005 ] FOSDEM 2005 is over

FOSDEM 2005 is now over. It was a big success, with many developers and talks, and a broad audience estimated between 3.000 and 3.500 people. We would like to thank all people involved in this big success, all those of you who have helped, who have donated, who have been present at the event. If you want to help us to improve FOSDEM organization, feel free to send your feedback.

We are now on the road to FOSDEM 2006 ...

[ 16-02-2005 ] Demonstration against software patents in Brussels

The FFII, which will hold a stand during the next FOSDEM, organises a demonstration against software patents in Brussels on thursday 17th february 2005. Although FOSDEM wants to stay away from any political involvement, we think it is important to support their action, and we encourage you to be present at the demonstration, if you want to continue what you enjoy the most : code and share your code freely.
