They promote Fosdem:

  • Cobbault homepage: A FOSDEMer homepage

  • Dagger: The Dagger project promotes us.

  • FREESCO: FREESCO sites (www.freesco.org & www.freesco.info) display our banner. FREESCO (stands for FREE ciSCO) is a free replacement for commercial routers supporting up to 3 ethernet/arcnet/token_ring/arlan network cards and up to 2 modems

  • GnomeMeeting: The ultimate video-conferencing solution

  • Infogroep: This students group is promoting FOSDEM ... but more important, they are coming to FOSDEM !

  • LinBoost: This belgian french-speaking news site displays our banner

  • Lumumba: The "Linux User Movement, Underground Mad Belgian Aliens" displays our banner

  • ProcoliX, Creative and professional ICT: This professional ICT has our banner.

  • RadioCentraal Webgang: WebGang is the IT program on an independent local radio station in Antwerp. Raphael Bauduin did a live interview in the studio on 12/02/2004 about Fosdem. The radio station is a project made and paid by more than 100 volunteers, no-one is getting paid and we do not broadcast commercials.

  • Tux Systems: A belgian user homepage is promoting FOSDEM

  • UK Free Software Network: UK Free Software Network is a UK ISP dedicated to the users of Free Software. In addition to providing all kinds of Internet access and hosting services, all of the profits from UKFSN are donated to support UK Free Software projects.

  • UnixTech: The Belgian Linux portal has our banner on rotation

  • WebGUI: The Dutch site of WebGUI, the Content Management System, has our banner.

  • Zeus: The Zeus working group promotes the FOSDEM as other meetings.
