The state of OpenJDK & OpenJDK6

A summary of the past year's accomplishments, some views on what remains to be done, and a look ahead to the content of JDK 7 and the process by which it will be developed.

And where are we with OpenJDK 6 today and where will we go tomorrow? The origins and initial design decision of the project will be discussed and well as possible future directions of the project.
